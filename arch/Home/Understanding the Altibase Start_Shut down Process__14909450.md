# Overview

---

This document describes the process of starting and shutting down Altibase, and describes the contents of the operation of the Altibase internal modules in each process and the tasks that users can perform.

※ The examples used in this document were performed in Altibase version 7.1.0, and the output result may differ depending on the Altibase version.

For errors and improvements related to this document, please contact the technical support portal or technical support center.
